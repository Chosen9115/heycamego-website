I believe we are living through a major transformation brought by AI. We now have digital intelligence on tap, and we’re still in the first minutes of understanding what this means. This is bigger than data centres, companies, or political views. The entire way society works will be changing.

Think about it: Homo sapiens built a “sapiens machine.” We used human intelligence to create digital intelligence, the very thing that made us different from other human species. That is mind-blowing. For the first time, we can build things and organisations that produce intelligent outputs and interact with the world. We get to experiment with what machines can do, rediscover where humans excel, and explore what new forms of organisation might emerge.

Nobody knows exactly where this leads, and that is why we should jump in and try things. We should not wait until the future pushes AI into our lives, like it did with computers and the internet. We have the chance to sail into the unknown and discover for ourselves what lies ahead.
